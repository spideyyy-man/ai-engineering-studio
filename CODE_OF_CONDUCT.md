# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want AI Engineering Studio to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience level, background or identity. Beginners asking questions are just as welcome as experienced engineers.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique code, not people.
- Assume good intent and help newcomers find their way.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues that break this code, and may block contributors who repeatedly or seriously violate it. To report a problem, contact the maintainers through a private channel on GitHub (for example, by messaging the repository owner, @spideyyy-man).

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
